Vice Cover Studio turns your photo and caption into GTA 6-style multi-panel poster art: bring up to three of your own photos (or use the two built-in samples), choose from four layouts and three Vice City colour grades, nudge each panel into place by dragging or with sliders, write your title and subtitle, then export a PNG at 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. Everything runs offline in your browser — photos never leave your machine — and every sunset, palm tree and letter is drawn by the app's own code. No Rockstar logos, artwork or fonts anywhere.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts --port 8080
# open http://localhost:8080
```
