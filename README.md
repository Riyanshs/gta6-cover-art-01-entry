Vice Cover Studio turns your photo and a caption into cover art in the style of GTA 6's multi-panel posters: pick up to three photos (or the two built-in samples), choose one of four layouts and three Vice City colour grades, crop and position each panel, edit the title and subtitle, and export a PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. Everything runs offline in the browser — photos never leave your machine. All artwork is drawn in code; no Rockstar logos, art or fonts.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts --port 8080
# open http://localhost:8080
```
